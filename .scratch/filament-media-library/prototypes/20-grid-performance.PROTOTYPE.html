Library Grid Performance Budget

Ticket 09 accepted one aggregate query per facet dimension per query change, with live counts computed across every filter except their own. Ticket 12 ruled that a card with no derivative yet renders a dimmed glyph tile, with no spinner and no polling. Neither said what that costs on a very large library or a slow connection. This demo simulates the server so you can feel it: type in the search box, flip facets, and watch when the grid paints, when the counts catch up, and how long the numbers on screen are lying.

Three decisions to make here. How long to wait after a keystroke before querying (the debounce). Whether the facet counts ride the same round trip as the results or trail behind them, and what gives way first when the budget blows. And whether a tiny blurred placeholder ships inside the grid payload so cards paint before their thumbnail arrives.

Card metadata is the id, name, filename, mime, visibility, dimensions, a duration for video and the derivative URL, budgeted at 220 bytes a card. Everything above that is the placeholder. The column that matters is the last one: what a person on hotel wifi waits before the first pixel of the batch appears.
